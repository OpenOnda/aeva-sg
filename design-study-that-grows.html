The Study That Grows

SpaceKids' study room
Ideal forHDB, condo & landed
MaterialsOak, pegboard, task light
Typical build3 to 4 weeks
The idea

One study for several children, built to adjust as they grow.

A long oak desk along the window with a zone for each child, each with adjustable shelf height and its own task lamp. Above it, a pegboard wall lets the kids change their layout every school term. Closed cabinets below keep school bags and art supplies out of sight, with a soft reading corner on the last wall.
