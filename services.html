Questions

Things parents usually ask.

How long does a renovation take?

A single kids' room usually takes 3 to 5 weeks from approved design to installation. A full HDB home is typically 8 to 12 weeks, and condos or landed homes 10 to 16 weeks, depending on scope.

Is everything really made in your own workshop?

Yes. Our carpenters fabricate in our own workshop, so we control the materials, the finish and the schedule. It's also why we can stand behind every piece with a 10-year carpentry warranty.

What materials do you use for kids' furniture?

Solid wood or high-grade plywood with real timber veneer, low-VOC finishes, rounded edges and soft-close hardware. We avoid sharp corners and anything that can trap small fingers.

Can you work with our existing furniture?

Of course. We'll plan around the pieces you love and design new carpentry to match.

Do you handle HDB permits?

Yes, permit applications are part of the project.

Can you help us order furniture from Taobao?

Yes. We'll help you shortlist pieces, check the measurements against your layout, arrange customisation where needed, and coordinate the ordering and delivery so it arrives when your home is ready for it.

Not sure where to start?

Send us your floor plan and a few photos. We'll suggest a direction, free.
