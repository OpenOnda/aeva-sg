The Treehouse Loft

SpaceShared kids' bedroom
Ideal forHDB BTO & resale
MaterialsWhite oak, warm strip light
Typical build3 to 5 weeks
The idea

For siblings sharing a small room. We build up instead of out.

A white oak loft bed with a sleeping deck above and a cosy den below, joined by a short ladder with wide, rounded rungs. A wall of shallow cubbies runs alongside the ladder so every toy has a home at kid height. Warm 2700K strip lights tucked under the deck make the den glow at story time without lighting up the whole room.
